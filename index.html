Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will the recipient know who sent the letter?

The intended experience is that your identity is not shared with the recipient. UNSENT may process information needed to fulfil an order. This prototype does not send letters yet.

What information do you need?

Online orders need a recipient email. Physical orders need a postal address. A sender email may be needed for order updates. Only collect information required to fulfil the order.

Are my letters stored securely?

This prototype does not upload or store your letter on a server. Before launch, private storage, access controls, retention and deletion rules, and secure upload handling must be implemented.

Is shipping included in the physical letter price?

Not yet specified. The physical letter is 50 lei; shipping must be configured and shown transparently before payment.
